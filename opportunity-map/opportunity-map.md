# Opportunity Map — Mada AlOmran

## Purpose

This file turns the market intelligence into an operational opportunity map.

The objective is to stop thinking only in terms of "leads" and start thinking in terms of identifiable commercial signals.

The basic unit is:

**Signal → Opportunity → Target → Access → Offer → Value → Priority → Next Action**

---

# 1. Opportunity Types

We will initially work with five opportunity types.

| ID | Opportunity | Primary Mada Service | Potential Expansion |
|---|---|---|---|
| O1 | Income-producing/rented building owner | Property Management | Maintenance, Facilities, Leasing, Sale |
| O2 | Old/underperforming building | Renovation / Contracting | Management, Development, Sale |
| O3 | Development-ready land/building | Development / Engineering | Contracting, Construction, Marketing, Sales |
| O4 | Investor/buyer | Asset Sales | Management, Leasing, Maintenance |
| O5 | Contracting/project opportunity | Contracting | Maintenance, Facilities, Development |

---

# 2. O1 — Income-Producing / Rented Building Owners

## Why this is first

This is the first prospecting engine because the owner already has an income-producing asset.

The sales conversation can therefore be based on improving the asset's operation and economics.

## Signals

High-value signals include:

- Fully rented
- Multiple apartments
- Annual income disclosed
- Existing electronic rental contracts
- Owner advertising directly
- Multiple utility meters
- Property described as investment
- Owner mentions income
- Building has recurring maintenance needs
- Building has vacancy or collection issues

Current Jeddah listings demonstrate that these signals are publicly visible. For example, one Al Bawadi listing describes 23 apartments, all rented on annual contracts, with current annual income of SAR 468,000. citeturn0search15

Another current Al Samer listing describes an 8-apartment building fully leased to a corporate tenant with annual income of SAR 400,000. citeturn0search3

## Qualification questions

Before outreach becomes a real opportunity, determine:

1. Is the person the owner or authorized decision maker?
2. Is the property currently self-managed?
3. Who handles rent collection?
4. Who handles maintenance?
5. Is there vacancy?
6. Are there collection problems?
7. Is there a maintenance backlog?
8. Is the owner considering selling?
9. Does the owner have other properties?
10. Would the owner consider full management?

## Offer

Do not start with:

"We are a property-management company."

Start with the owner's economics:

**"We can take over the operational management of the property and help organize leasing, collections, maintenance, and day-to-day property operations."**

The exact commercial offer must later be adapted to the owner's actual pain.

## Expansion

Management can lead to:

**Management → Maintenance → Facilities → Renovation → Sale/Development**

## Priority

**Very High**

# 3. O2 — Old / Underperforming Buildings

## Signals

- Age 30+ years
- "Old building"
- "Demolition and reconstruction"
- "Renovation needed"
- Low income relative to asset potential
- Vacant units
- Partial completion
- Poor condition
- Owner explicitly describes redevelopment opportunity

A current Al Sulaymaniyah listing describes a 625 m², 6-apartment building as old and an investment opportunity for demolition and reconstruction. citeturn0search12

A current As Safaa listing describes a 625 m², 46-year-old property with 6 apartments and 2 shops, annual income of SAR 186,000, and explicitly positions it as suitable for demolition and redevelopment. citeturn0search13

## Qualification questions

1. What is the owner's goal?
2. Keep and renovate?
3. Demolish and rebuild?
4. Sell?
5. Increase income?
6. Is there financing available?
7. Is the property occupied?
8. What is the current annual income?
9. What is the owner's expected timeline?
10. Is the owner open to a development/contracting proposal?

## Offer

The initial offer should be diagnostic:

**Property assessment → improvement/redevelopment options → estimated commercial outcome → execution path**

Only then should we propose the appropriate service.

## Expansion

**Assessment → Engineering → Renovation/Contracting → Development → Marketing/Sales → Management**

## Priority

**High**

# 4. O3 — Development-Ready Land / Buildings

## Signals

- Vacant land
- Demolition-ready building
- Partially completed project
- Development/investment language
- Strategic frontage
- Suitable plot dimensions
- Owner unable/unwilling to execute development alone

A current Al Bawadi listing describes a 546 m² property with an old two-story building, explicitly marketed as a redevelopment opportunity for investors and developers. citeturn0search14

## Qualification questions

1. Does the owner want to sell or develop?
2. What is the permitted development?
3. What is the owner's budget?
4. Is the owner willing to finance?
5. Does the owner need a development partner?
6. What exit strategy does the owner prefer?
7. Is the owner seeking rental income or sale?
8. What is the expected timeline?

## Offer

Do not sell construction first.

Sell the **decision path**:

**Feasibility → Development concept → Economics → Execution option**

If the project makes commercial sense, the company can then connect the opportunity to engineering, contracting, construction, marketing and sales.

## Expansion

**Landowner → Feasibility → Development → Contracting → Construction → Sales → Management**

## Priority

**High value / lower volume**

# 5. O4 — Investors and Buyers

## Signals

- Searching for investment buildings
- Comparing rental yields
- Looking for ready units
- Looking for multiple units
- Asking about annual income
- Seeking development land
- Seeking discounted assets

## Qualification

Identify:

- Investment budget
- Preferred area
- Property type
- Cash/financing
- Desired yield
- Hold period
- Management requirement
- Development appetite

## Offer

Match the investor to:

**Company inventory + investment logic + post-sale services**

The relationship should not end at the sale.

## Expansion

**Sale → Management → Leasing → Maintenance → Future acquisition**

## Priority

**High when the investor is qualified**

# 7. Opportunity Score — Version 1

We will use a simple score before building automation.

## Positive signals

- Direct owner / decision maker identified: +25
- Multiple units: +15
- Existing rental income: +15
- Fully rented: +10
- Clear problem/need: +20
- Development/renovation signal: +15
- Strong fit with Mada capabilities: +15
- More than one possible service: +10
- Active timing: +10

## Negative signals

- Professional property manager already in place: -15
- Decision maker inaccessible: -20
- No identifiable need: -15
- Outside company capability: -25
- Very long/uncertain timing: -10

## Initial interpretation

- **80+** = Priority A
- **60–79** = Priority B
- **40–59** = Monitor
- **Below 40** = Low priority

This is a prototype.

We will calibrate it using actual results.

# 8. Access Route

The opportunity map must distinguish between discovering a property and reaching its decision maker.

## Route A — Public listing contact

Use the contact route provided by the platform or advertiser.

## Route B — Existing relationship

Use:

- Existing clients
- Referrals
- Professional network
- Business introductions

## Route C — Company/public business contact

Use official business contact channels where available.

## Route D — Formal procurement platform

For:

- Muqawil
- Etimad
- Other official procurement channels

We do not use private personal data improperly.

# 10. First Manual Experiment

We should not automate yet.

The first experiment should be manual.

## Target

Build a small sample of **20 opportunities** from Jeddah public listings and relationships.

Suggested distribution:

- 10 rented/income-producing buildings
- 5 old/underperforming buildings
- 3 development-ready assets
- 2 contracting/project opportunities

For each, record the opportunity structure above.

## Then measure

- How many have an identifiable decision maker?
- How many have a clear need?
- How many are reachable?
- How many accept contact?
- How many meetings result?
- How many proposals result?
- Which opportunity type converts best?

Only after this experiment should we decide whether automation/AI is justified.

---

# 11. Important Discovery

The opportunity is not the property listing itself.

The listing is only a **signal**.

The actual opportunity is:

**A decision maker + a real need + a service fit + reachable access + timing**

This distinction will prevent us from building a huge list of useless leads.

---

# 12. Commercial Flywheel From Opportunities

Every successful opportunity should be checked for secondary revenue.

### Example

**Rented building owner**

→ Property Management

→ Maintenance

→ Facilities

→ Renovation

→ Contracting

→ Sale / Development

This is why opportunity quality matters more than raw lead quantity.

## Compliance Note

Property-management activity in Saudi Arabia is a regulated activity. REGA states that the FAL property-management license authorizes establishments to manage the financial and administrative affairs of property on behalf of others, while the facility-management license covers technical affairs. citeturn0search0turn0search4

Therefore, prospecting and outreach should lead to a properly structured licensed company service/contract, not informal property-management activity.
