# Al-Ruwais Field Sheet V1

## Purpose

This is the first manual field-capture form for the Al-Ruwais target-area campaign.

Its purpose is simple:

> **Turn every useful field interaction into a structured commercial opportunity that can be followed up and measured.**

No automation is required at this stage.

# Operating rule

Do not try to fill every field during the first conversation.

The field representative's priority is:

**Reach → Understand → Qualify → Capture → Follow up.**

Only collect information that helps move the opportunity forward.

---

# Learning loop

After every meaningful batch of field interactions:

**Build → Understand → Test → Error → Understand the Cause → Fix → Review**

Field data is used to improve:

- target-area selection
- qualification questions
- service positioning
- sales approach
- opportunity scoring
- follow-up process
- revenue measurement
