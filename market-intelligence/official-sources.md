# Official Sources Registry

## Purpose

This document defines the official and high-priority sources that the Mada AlOmran Revenue Engine should return to repeatedly when researching real-estate, demolition, rehabilitation, construction, procurement, licensing, and related commercial opportunities in Jeddah and Saudi Arabia.

These sources are part of the project's permanent research workflow. They are not one-time references.

## Source hierarchy

### Tier 1 — Official government sources

1. **Jeddah Municipality (أمانة محافظة جدة)**
   - Role: official local source for Jeddah municipal programs, neighborhood removal/rehabilitation signals, municipal services, and local building-related information.
   - Website: https://www.jeddah.gov.sa/
   - Use when: identifying target areas and verifying official municipal signals before field coverage.

2. **Balady Platform (منصة بلدي)**
   - Role: official Saudi municipal platform for building and municipal services.
   - Website: https://balady.gov.sa/
   - Key research areas:
     - building permits
     - demolition permits
     - renovation permits
     - technical reports
     - surveying reports
     - building-permit inquiry
   - Use when: validating building activity, permits, and regulatory/service signals.

3. **Etimad Platform (منصة اعتماد)**
   - Role: official Saudi government procurement and tenders platform.
   - Website: https://tenders.etimad.sa/
   - Use when: identifying public contracting, demolition, removal, construction, rehabilitation, engineering, and infrastructure opportunities.

4. **Saudi Exchange / Tadawul (تداول السعودية)**
   - Role: official Saudi financial-market disclosure source.
   - Website: https://www.saudiexchange.sa/
   - Use when: verifying public-company disclosures, ownership/leadership information, and material corporate announcements when relevant.

5. **Saudi Press Agency (واس / SPA)**
   - Role: official Saudi news agency.
   - Website: https://www.spa.gov.sa/
   - Use when: verifying officially reported government decisions, programs, municipal initiatives, and major developments.

6. **Ministry of Municipalities and Housing (وزارة البلديات والإسكان)**
   - Role: official national source for municipal/housing regulations, programs, guides, and policy information.
   - Website: https://momah.gov.sa/
   - Use when: understanding the regulatory framework behind municipal/building activity.

7. **Real Estate General Authority (الهيئة العامة للعقار - REGA)**
   - Role: official Saudi real-estate regulator.
   - Website: https://rega.gov.sa/
   - Use when: verifying real-estate regulatory requirements, licensing, brokerage/property-management rules, and related compliance.

## Tier 2 — Official / industry platforms used for commercial validation

8. **Muqawil (مقاول)**
   - Role: Saudi Contractors Authority platform / contractor ecosystem.
   - Website: https://muqawil.org/
   - Use when: validating contracting-market activity, contractor capabilities, and potential contracting opportunities.

## Research rule

For any important commercial conclusion, classify the evidence as:

- **Official** — directly supported by an official government/regulatory source.
- **Company-confirmed** — confirmed by Mada AlOmran.
- **Market evidence** — supported by reputable market/listing/industry sources.
- **Inference** — our interpretation based on available evidence.
- **Unknown** — not yet verified.

An official source confirms the existence/status of an official signal; it does **not** automatically prove that every property or owner inside an area has the same status.

## Permanent workflow

Before adopting a target area or major opportunity:

**Discover → Verify Official Source → Record Evidence → Classify Opportunity → Field Test → Measure → Review**

The project should revisit these sources continuously as part of Market Intelligence and Opportunity Discovery.

## Current strategic use

The first field strategy focuses on Jeddah areas with credible official signals related to removal, unsafe buildings, rehabilitation, demolition, or redevelopment.

The area is treated as a **target market**, not as proof that every individual property has a removal order.

Field work then validates individual buildings, owners/decision makers, needs, and next actions through lawful and respectful commercial contact.

## Important note

Government websites, services, and URLs can change. Before relying on a specific page, verify that it is currently official and accessible. Store the source's purpose and official domain here so the workflow remains reusable even if a specific service page changes.
