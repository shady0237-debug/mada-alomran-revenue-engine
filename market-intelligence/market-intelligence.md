# Market Intelligence — Jeddah Opportunity Pools

## Purpose

This file begins the market-intelligence phase of the Mada AlOmran Revenue Engine.

The goal is not to collect market facts for their own sake.

The goal is to identify:

**Where the opportunity is → Who controls it → What problem exists → Which Mada AlOmran service fits → How difficult it is to access → How it can become revenue**

---

# 1. Current Market Signal

The official Real Estate General Authority (REGA) indicators show substantial real-estate transaction activity in Jeddah.

The current official Jeddah snapshot shows approximately:

- 9.2K sale transactions
- SAR 14.3B total transaction value
- As Safa as the most active district in the displayed indicator
- Apartments as the most active property type

The data is sourced from the Ministry of Justice and the Real Estate Register.

**Conclusion:** Jeddah is not a market lacking transactions. The commercial challenge is finding the right opportunity and reaching the right decision maker.

---

# 2. Building-Sale Opportunity Pool

Aqar's current Jeddah building-for-sale pages show roughly:

- 865–871 building listings across Jeddah in recent crawls
- Around 770+ listings in North Jeddah
- Around 90+ listings in South Jeddah

The exact count changes as listings are added/removed, so these numbers should be treated as a current market snapshot rather than a permanent statistic.

Listings frequently expose useful commercial signals such as:

- Land area
- Number of apartments
- Building age
- Annual income
- Occupancy/rental status
- Development potential
- Whether the advertiser states a direct owner relationship
- Asking price

**Conclusion:** Public listings provide a large, structured opportunity pool from which property-management, maintenance, renovation, development, and contracting signals can be extracted.

---

# 3. The Three Priority Opportunity Pools

We will compare three pools first because they match Mada AlOmran's capabilities particularly well.

## Pool A — Owners of Rented Buildings

### Typical signal

Examples found in current Jeddah listings include:

- Multiple apartments
- Existing annual rental income
- Fully rented buildings
- Electronic rental contracts
- Investment-property positioning

One current Aqar example in As Safa describes a 1,250 m² building with 24 apartments and current income of SAR 480,000, with the listing suggesting higher potential income.

Another Al Bawadi example describes a 525 m² building with 7 apartments and annual income of SAR 160,000.

These are exactly the kinds of signals that can indicate a property-management opportunity, even when the original listing is for sale.

### Best Mada service

**Property Management**

### Secondary services

- Maintenance
- Facility management
- Leasing
- Renovation
- Sale
- Future development

### Opportunity size

**High**

There are many buildings with multiple units and existing income.

### Access friction

**Medium**

The decision maker may be reachable through the listing channel, but the owner/decision maker must be identified and qualified.

### Commercial attractiveness

**Very High**

One owner can produce recurring management revenue plus maintenance and other services.

### Strategic assessment

**Priority #1 for systematic prospecting.**

---

# Pool B — Old / Underperforming / Development-Ready Buildings

### Typical signal

Public listings show examples of:

- 20–40+ year-old buildings
- Buildings needing renovation
- Buildings suitable for demolition and reconstruction
- Partially completed buildings
- Properties where income is below potential
- Buildings with development potential

For example, current Jeddah listings include an Al Faisaliyah property described as old and suitable for renovation or demolition/reconstruction.

Other listings explicitly market properties as redevelopment opportunities.

### Best Mada service

**Renovation / Rehabilitation / Contracting / Development**

### Secondary services

- Engineering
- Property management
- Maintenance
- Marketing
- Sale

### Opportunity size

**High**

Jeddah has a large existing building stock and a visible market of older and underperforming assets.

### Access friction

**Medium to High**

The technical opportunity can be valuable, but the sales cycle may require:

- Owner identification
- Site assessment
- Engineering analysis
- Cost estimation
- Feasibility
- Financing discussion
- Decision-maker approval

### Commercial attractiveness

**Very High per successful opportunity**

A single opportunity can generate a much larger project value than a normal brokerage transaction.

### Strategic assessment

**Priority #2, with high strategic value.**

This pool should be developed through a specialized development/contracting offer rather than a generic sales pitch.

---

# Pool C — Land / Building Owners Suitable for Development

### Typical signal

- Vacant land
- Partially completed buildings
- Owner-built properties
- Properties with development potential
- Properties where the owner has an asset but lacks development/contracting execution

Current Aqar listings include multiple Jeddah properties marketed as development, completion, or reconstruction opportunities.

### Best Mada service

**Development / Engineering / Contracting**

### Secondary services

- Marketing
- Sales
- Property management

### Opportunity size

**High**

The value of a successful development relationship can be substantial.

### Access friction

**High**

Development decisions are usually more complex than management decisions.

The owner may need to agree on:

- Feasibility
- Design
- Budget
- Financing
- Construction
- Timeline
- Exit strategy

### Commercial attractiveness

**Very High**

But sales-cycle length and execution complexity are higher.

### Strategic assessment

**Priority #3 for systematic acquisition, but potentially the highest-value relationship.**

# 5. Why Rented Buildings Come First

A rented building has an important commercial advantage:

**The owner already has an income-producing asset.**

That means the conversation can focus on improving:

- Income
- Occupancy
- Collection
- Maintenance
- Operating costs
- Tenant stability
- Asset value

This creates a much stronger management proposition than simply saying:

"We manage properties."

The eventual sales message should be based on measurable owner outcomes.

---

# 6. Why Old Buildings Are a Second Engine

An old building can create several simultaneous needs:

**Management + Maintenance + Renovation + Contracting + Development + Sale**

The opportunity is therefore not just a maintenance contract.

The right owner may become a multi-service client.

This fits the company's integrated capabilities particularly well.

---

# 7. Why Development Is the High-Value Long Game

A landowner or building owner may represent a complete project rather than a single transaction.

Potential chain:

**Owner → Feasibility → Engineering → Development → Contracting → Construction → Marketing → Sales → Management**

The access barrier is higher, but the relationship can produce multiple revenue events.

This should be developed as a relationship-driven business-development channel rather than a mass cold-sales campaign.

---

# 8. Contracting / Construction Opportunity Pool

The Saudi Contractors Authority's Muqawil platform currently shows a large project market.

The current platform snapshot shows approximately:

- 1,745 project and job opportunities
- Estimated project value of approximately SAR 2.36B
- 274 alliance opportunities

The Projects Platform is designed to connect project owners with qualified contractors and covers activities including building construction, civil engineering, specialized construction, and building services.

The platform requires a valid contractor membership for contractors submitting bids.

**Commercial implication:** Contracting opportunities should not depend only on personal networking. There is an existing structured digital opportunity source that can be monitored and qualified.

---

# 9. Government Contracting Opportunity Pool

Etimad's current public portal shows a large government tender environment.

A current snapshot shows approximately:

- 34K active tenders
- 2K awarded tenders
- 20K offered proposals

The platform's activity categories include large volumes of:

- Operations, maintenance and cleaning
- General building contracting
- Construction, repair, demolition and restoration
- General construction

Jeddah-specific examples currently visible include building operation/maintenance/cleaning opportunities and maintenance/operation of facilities.

**Commercial implication:** Government contracting is a real opportunity pool, but it should be treated as a qualification-heavy channel because eligibility, classification, documentation, technical capacity, guarantees, and bidding requirements can create significant access friction.

# 13. Access-Friction Model

We will not judge opportunities by value alone.

Each opportunity should eventually be scored on:

### Opportunity Value

How much revenue could this relationship generate?

### Access

Can we actually reach the decision maker?

### Need

Is there a real problem or merely a generic property listing?

### Fit

Does Mada AlOmran have the right capability?

### Timing

Is the need active now?

### Expansion

Can one deal lead to additional services?

The best opportunity is not necessarily the biggest property.

It is the opportunity with the best combination of:

**Value + Need + Access + Fit + Timing + Expansion**

# 15. What We Do NOT Do Yet

We do not immediately start scraping thousands of listings.

We first need to define the exact commercial qualification rules.

We also do not contact everyone.

We prioritize.

We do not build AI automation yet.

First we manually prove that the opportunity model works.

Only after the manual process creates enough volume do we automate the repetitive parts.
