# Master Workflow — Mada AlOmran Revenue Engine

## 1. Purpose

This document is the master workflow for the Mada AlOmran Revenue Engine project.

It records how we will understand the company, discover commercial opportunities, convert those opportunities into business, measure results, and continuously improve the system.

This is a living document. When we learn something important, test an assumption, discover an error, or change the strategy based on evidence, this workflow should be reviewed and updated.

---

## 2. Core Objective

Our objective is not simply to sell the company's current properties.

The objective is to increase the flow of valuable business opportunities into Mada AlOmran and convert those opportunities into measurable revenue.

The company is treated as an integrated revenue ecosystem:

**Asset Sales ↔ Property Management ↔ Contracting & Construction ↔ Development ↔ Maintenance & Facilities Management**

A single relationship may create more than one commercial opportunity.

Examples:

- Property owner → property management → maintenance → renovation → contracting → sale/development.
- Buyer → purchase → property management/leasing → maintenance → future investment.
- Landowner → engineering/development → contracting → marketing/sales → management.
- Owner not ready to sell → management/leasing now → sale or development later.

---

## 3. Commercial Mindset

The role is not limited to being a traditional salesperson.

The working mindset is:

**Discover where money and opportunity exist inside the company's capabilities, connect the right customer to the right service, and turn the relationship into revenue.**

The official job title may differ, but the operating objective is revenue and opportunity development.

---

## 4. Master Workflow

We will follow this sequence:

**Company Intelligence → Asset Map → Market Intelligence → Opportunity Map → Sales System → Measurement → Improvement**

These stages are connected. We do not jump randomly between them.

# Phase 1 — Company Intelligence

## Objective

Understand what Mada AlOmran actually is, what it can legally and commercially do, what assets and clients it already has, and where its current capabilities are underused.

## We study

- Company identity
- Ownership and leadership
- Licenses and qualifications
- Services
- Real estate brokerage and marketing
- Property management
- Facility management
- Contracting and construction
- Maintenance
- Development
- Engineering and consulting
- Auctions and related services
- Existing clients
- Existing contracts
- Current advertising and lead sources
- Existing sales activity
- Existing operational capacity
- Internal strengths and constraints

## Evidence rule

Every important fact should be classified as:

- **Confirmed independently** — supported by an external reliable source.
- **Confirmed by company** — published or provided by the company.
- **Inference** — conclusion derived from several facts.
- **Unknown** — requires internal confirmation.

We do not turn assumptions into facts.

## Current internal facts already established

- Mada AlOmran currently manages at least 4 properties in Jeddah for external owners.
- Management is full property management, not only rent collection.
- One of the managed properties is a small compound.
- The company also has some small maintenance/facility contracts.
- The company has current properties/projects being marketed.
- The company wants to increase investment and business opportunities through its available capabilities.
- Current customers appear to come significantly through the company's existing advertising activity; the exact percentage is not yet established.

These facts can be updated as the user provides more internal information.

# Phase 5 — Opportunity Engine

## Objective

Turn public and relationship-based signals into prioritized commercial opportunities.

## Conceptual flow

**Public Sources / Networks**

↓

**Property or Business Signal**

↓

**Asset Data Extraction**

↓

**Owner / Asset Classification**

↓

**Opportunity Score**

↓

**Best Mada AlOmran Service**

↓

**Compliant Outreach**

↓

**Qualification**

↓

**Meeting**

↓

**Proposal**

↓

**Contract**

↓

**Revenue**

↓

**Cross-Sell**

## Example

A public property listing may show:

- 600 m² building
- 11 units
- Fully rented
- Annual income disclosed
- Direct with owner

Even if the owner originally wants to sell, the same signal may represent a property-management opportunity.

The system should identify the hidden commercial opportunity rather than only the original advertisement purpose.

## Opportunity Score — prototype

This scoring model is only a starting hypothesis and must be calibrated using real company results.

Possible signals:

- Direct with owner: +30
- More than 5 units: +20
- Fully rented: +15
- Annual income disclosed: +15
- Older building: +10
- Renovation/development need: +10
- Professional developer/internal management already present: -20

Initial interpretation:

- 80–100: High priority
- 60–79: Worth contacting
- 40–59: Monitor
- Below 40: Low priority

We will change these weights when actual conversion data proves a better model.

For every opportunity source we document:

2. What signal identifies it
3. Who the decision maker is
4. How the decision maker can be reached through a legitimate channel
5. What initial message/offer is appropriate
6. What service is most relevant
7. Estimated opportunity value
8. Next action
9. Possible second service
10. Possible third service

We use public information and legitimate business contact channels.

We do not collect or use private personal data improperly.

# Phase 7 — Sales System

## Objective

Create a measurable pipeline rather than relying on memory or random follow-up.

## Pipeline

**Lead → Qualified → Meeting → Proposal → Negotiation → Won/Lost → Revenue**

For every opportunity we should eventually be able to answer:

- Who is the customer?
- What asset/business need exists?
- What service are we offering?
- What is the estimated value?
- What is the current stage?
- What is the next action?
- When is the next action?
- What happened?
- Why was it won or lost?
- What other service could follow?

# Phase 9 — AI & Tools

## Rule

Technology is introduced only when a real bottleneck exists.

We do not use AI, Python, automation, APIs, webhooks, MCP, databases, or other tools merely because they are available.

## Potential future uses

AI may help with:

- Reading public listings
- Extracting structured property information
- Classifying assets
- Classifying owner/business type
- Scoring opportunities
- Suggesting the most relevant company service
- Prioritizing outreach
- Preparing research
- Summarizing conversations and notes
- Identifying cross-sell opportunities

Automation may later help with:

- Repetitive data collection
- Follow-up
- Reporting
- Alerts
- Pipeline updates

The tool must solve a demonstrated problem.

# Phase 11 — Continuous Improvement

The system is never considered finished.

We repeatedly ask:

1. What did we build?
2. What did we expect?
3. What actually happened?
4. Where did the process fail?
5. Why did it fail?
6. What should change?
7. What should we test next?
8. Did the change improve the result?

This is the commercial version of our learning loop:

**Build → Understand → Test → Error → Understand the Cause → Fix → Review**

---

# 12. Project Rules

1. Evidence before assumptions.
2. Real business need before tools.
3. Do not confuse public marketing information with proven operational facts.
4. Do not repeatedly verify facts already confirmed internally unless new evidence creates a reason.
5. Keep the workflow stable unless evidence shows that it needs to change.
6. Record important decisions and changes.
7. Measure results whenever possible.
8. Use legitimate and compliant sources and outreach channels.
9. Keep the project practical and connected to real revenue.
10. Learn the technology while solving real commercial problems.
